#### Editorializing

In formal research writing, you would not include editorializing statements such as:
  
  “Most of the time it wasn’t accidental”
  
  “The consequences have been serious”

  For example, in the first case, you could say, “Historically, researchers intentionally excluded female subjects…”

#### Contracting
  
  Do not contract your words in formal writing (i.e., wasn’t should be was not).
  
#### Undefined acronym
  
  PCOS

#### Missing citations

“Conditions like endometriosis, uterine fibroids, autoimmune disorders and PCOS, remain underfunded and understudied relative to their prevalence.”

There is a citation in the previous sentence and two sentences later, but the source for this sentence is unclear.  
Be sure to include a citation when making a claim that cannot be supported by general knowledge.

I leave it to you to audit the rest of your claims for required citations.
